RULES FOR BUILDERS

  1. Home has three cards and nothing else: Kiska bill · Bheje hue bills · Kiske paise aane hain.
  2. One gradient hero per screen, at most one secondary panel. Everything else is white on the dot-grid ground.
  3. Saffron is for the single primary action and the active tab. Nowhere else.
  4. Hero money = Urbanist 500 figures with a lighter ₹ (300). Lists and tables use 15/600 tabular figures.
  5. Red always comes with a word (Baaki, 24 din). Never colour alone.
  6. WhatsApp green appears only on the WhatsApp button.
  7. The app speaks Hinglish. The bill PDF speaks formal English: no gradients, no Doto, no Hinglish.
  8. Every string comes from the copy dictionary. No hard-coded labels.
  9. Touch targets ≥ 44px. Text on gradients meets 4.5:1, or 3:1 at 24px+.
