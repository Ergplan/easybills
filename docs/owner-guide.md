# Using EasyBills

A short guide to the three things you will do most: make a bill, repeat it every
month, and record a payment.

---

## Before your first bill

You can start typing a bill straight away. Before you **issue** your first one,
we need two things — tap **Finish business setup** on Home, or the gear icon.

**Your GST status.** Choose one:

- *Not registered for GST* — no GST is added to your bills.
- *Regular GST* — you have a GST number and add GST. We will also ask for your
  yearly sales, and ask you to confirm that the government e-invoice system does
  not apply to you. We cannot check that for you, so please ask your accountant
  if you are not sure.
- *Composition scheme* — not supported yet. You can keep drafts, but not issue.
- *Not sure* — you can keep drafts. Find out before you issue, so your bill is
  correct.

**Your bill numbers.** Your bills are numbered in order and start again each
financial year. If you already issue bills elsewhere this year, set the prefix
and starting number here so your numbers do not clash. Tick the box that says
you bill elsewhere and we will keep out of the way.

Everything else — address, logo, bank details, UPI — is optional and can wait.

## Make a bill

**Home → Create bill.** Then choose:

- **Quick bill** — for a walk-in. No customer details needed. No customer record
  is created. If you want a name or phone on the bill, or you want to chase it
  later, tap *Add details*.
- **Customer invoice** — for someone you want to keep a record for, chase, or
  bill every month.

Then:

1. **Customer.** Search your saved customers, or tap *+ Add a new customer*. A
   name is all we need. Address, phone and GST number appear only when they
   matter. A customer with no GST number is perfectly normal.
2. **Items.** Type what you are billing for, the quantity (it starts at 1) and
   the price. Tap *+ Add another item* for more. If you charge GST, choose the
   rate for each item — we do not guess it for you, and we will not let you
   issue a bill with the rate left blank. If an item really is nil-rated,
   choose 0% so the bill says you meant it. Your saved items appear as
   buttons above the first line; tap one to fill it in.
3. **More, on any item.** Discount, unit, HSN code, "the price already includes
   GST", and *Save this item for next time*. Items are only saved when you ask.
4. **Where is this supply for?** If you charge GST, confirm the state. We
   suggest one from the customer's details, but an address is not a tax rule, so
   please check it.
5. **More options.** Bill date, when payment is due, and a note for the customer.
6. **Review.** The total at the bottom of the screen follows you as you type.
   Tap **Review** to see the full bill and the exact figures.
7. **Issue invoice.** This is the moment the bill becomes real: it gets its
   number and can no longer be edited.

Your work saves as you go. The small line next to the total tells you where it
is:

| It says | It means |
|---|---|
| Saving… | On its way to us |
| Saved | We have it |
| Saved on this device — waiting for internet | Your phone has it, we do not yet. It will go when you are back online |
| Changed somewhere else — refresh to see it | You have this bill open somewhere else too |

### If we cannot issue it

Sometimes the review screen says we cannot issue a bill — a GST status that is
not settled, or a kind of bill this app does not handle yet. **Your draft is
saved and nothing is lost.** The message says what is needed and what you can do.

## Share it, and get paid

On an issued bill:

- **Share PDF** hands the bill to your phone's own share options — WhatsApp,
  email, whatever you use. We cannot tell whether you actually sent it, so we
  will not pretend the bill was delivered, and it stays unpaid until you record
  a payment.
- **View PDF** and **Download** give you the file.

Your customer needs no account to open the PDF.

If you have added a UPI ID in settings, a payment QR code appears on the bill.
Check your UPI ID carefully — a wrong one sends your customer's money to someone
else. That QR is for payment only; it is not a government e-invoice QR code.

### Payment received

Tap **Payment received** and enter the amount, the date and how it was paid.
Enter part of it and the bill shows **Part paid** with the remainder.

A scanned QR, a screenshot or a message saying "sent" is not payment. Record it
when the money has actually reached you.

**Amount deducted** is different, and has its own tab in the same panel. Use it
when a customer paid you less than the bill because they held something back,
such as TDS. It reduces what is still to collect, it is **not** counted as cash,
and it does not change the bill or the GST on it.

**Made a mistake?** Every payment can be reversed. Tap *Reverse* next to it and
say why. The original entry stays in the history — you will see that money came
in and was later reversed, which is what you want if anyone ever asks.

### If something was wrong

An issued bill cannot be edited — that is deliberate, because your customer
already has it. Instead, tap **Raise a credit or debit note** on the bill:

- *Customer owes less* — a credit note. Use it if you overcharged, billed
  something twice, or agreed a reduction afterwards.
- *Customer owes more* — a debit note.

Say why. The reason is kept and printed on the note. The original bill stays
exactly as it was, and the note is linked to it, so anyone looking later can see
both.

If you charge GST there is one more question: **does this change the GST you
owe?** Leave it unticked if you are only adjusting what your customer owes you.
They are not the same thing, and only that box affects your return. Ask your
accountant if you are unsure.

## Repeat it every month

On a bill for a customer you bill regularly, turn on **Repeat every month**.

We will ask:

- **When to prepare the next draft** — a day of the month. Choose 31 and short
  months use their last day, then it goes back to 31.
- **What the bill covers** — this month, or last month. We show you an example
  so there is no guessing.
- **When to stop**, if you know.

Then every month we prepare a **draft** and it appears on Home under *Monthly
bills ready*. Nothing is sent to your customer, and nothing is issued, until you
look at it and tap Issue.

This runs on our side, so it works whether or not you have the app open.

**Changing a monthly bill.** By default a change applies to *this bill only*.
If you want it to stick, choose *This and future invoices* — we will show you
what changes and from which month, before anything is committed. A price you
have already agreed does not move just because you change a saved item later.

**Pause, skip, resume, stop.** All four are there. Skipping one month really
skips it — resuming later does not sneak it back in. Pausing and resuming picks
up from the next date, not by back-filling everything you missed.

## Find things again

**Bills** has a search box (customer, number or date) and filters for Draft,
Unpaid and Paid. Overdue bills are marked.

**Duplicate as a new bill** on any bill makes a fresh draft with the same items.
It does not copy the number, the payment status, or a monthly schedule.

**Customers** shows what each customer owes you, what is overdue, and any money
they have paid in advance that is not yet applied to a bill.

Changing a customer's details affects future bills only. Bills already issued
keep the details they were issued with — that is deliberate.

## Speak or type your bill

Inside the bill editor there is a button: **Speak or type your bill**. Say or
type something like:

> Bill Sharma Electricals for two repair visits at 800 each and spare parts of 450

and we will fill in the form for you to check. It is a shortcut for typing, not
a replacement — everything appears in the ordinary form, and you review and
issue it exactly as usual.

- If we are not sure which customer you meant, we ask.
- If you did not say a price, we leave it blank. We never guess an amount.
- It can fill in the form. It cannot issue a bill, send anything, record a
  payment, or change your bank details.
- If it fails or is slow, nothing you typed is lost and you carry on by hand.

For voice, tap **Record**, speak, then **Stop**. We show you what we heard so
you can correct it before it is used. If your phone will not give us the
microphone, typing keeps working.
